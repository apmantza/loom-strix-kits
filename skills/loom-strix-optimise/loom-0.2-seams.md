# Loom SDK seams our kernels could use (hrx-loom 0.2.0 vs upstream main)

Read-only survey, 2026-11-21, against the 0.2.0 tag (0cd370f2ba); every "expected effect" is a doc claim until a compile report
or a timing shows it. 0.3.0 (2026-11-21) compiles our attention and GEMM kernels byte-identically (re-check per kernel).
Paths: D = hrx-system/loom/docs/src, S = hrx-system/loom/src/loom, P = hrx-system/loom/py/loom (an hrx-system checkout at the tag),
L = ROCm/hrx-loom-library at dd23d2f (its Loom pin is the 0.2.0 tag's parent, so its motifs match), K = this repo's kit/.
Kernels: (1) flash2 prefill attention, (2) prefill GEMMs, (3) vsplit verify attention, (4) decode matvecs and small producers.

What our generators use today (grep of K): no `pipeline(...)`, no `unroll(%factor)`, no `schedule(...)` loop policy, no
Low helpers. `scf.schedule.fence` appears only in gen_pstep.py:60,76 (the quantize helper). gen_flash2s.py uses bare
`unroll` (line 356) only. gen_gemm.py has hand prefetch, `vector.load`+`vector.fragment<rhs>` from LDS, padded x72 stages,
and only an orientation swap for traversal (GEMM_N_FASTEST, gen_gemm.py:1344-1349).

## Ranked: try first

1. `scf.schedule.fence` in flash2 (and vsplit): replace the bit-pattern select with fence / QK+softmax / fence / V consume; zero SDK cost, removes an artificial dependency, check with load_cover.py and the wait_action_rows report.
2. Evaluate main for #3419 + #3442 (needs an SDK upgrade or source build): they target the exact register-allocation early waits of #3409 that cap our register prefetch in flash2 and the GEMM back-edge; first check by compiling the two kernels with main and diffing waits/VGPRs, no GPU needed.
3. GEMM: port the library linear_temporal pattern (paired RHS LDS loads retired at fences with read-ahead, two LDS banks, one barrier per K64) for the lgkmcnt(0)-after-every-pair serialization; it is the closest shipped gfx11 WMMA schedule to ours.
4. GEMM 5120x6144 outlier: grouped traversal (linear_tiled_coordinates, group size sweep) is a cheap one-ingredient experiment; we currently only swap orientation.
5. vsplit: `pipeline(%depth) unroll(%factor)` over a fixed inner row tile (cooperative paged-attention pattern) vs fence-staged ID/payload distances; report-first (`suggest`, `scf.compare_pipeline_depth`, `amdgpu.pipeline_copy_waits`).

Dead ends on gfx1151 in 0.2.0 (do not spend time): VGPR cap/occupancy knobs (none), cache policy hints (dropped), async
global->LDS copy (rejected), `view.prefetch` (gfx12 `s_prefetch_data`), split barriers (full-barrier fallback), scheduler
strategy (fixed).
